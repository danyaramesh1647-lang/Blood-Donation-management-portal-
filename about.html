About BloodConnect

BloodConnect is a community platform that connects voluntary blood donors with patients and hospitals in need of urgent blood supply. Our mission is to make blood donation simple, fast, and accessible for everyone.


Whether you want to register as a donor, search for donors nearby, submit an emergency request, or find a donation camp near you — BloodConnect brings it all together in one place.
